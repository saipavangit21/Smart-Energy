## GMass Setup Instructions

1. Upload `ev_outreach_contacts.csv` to Google Sheets
2. Install GMass at gmass.co (free for 50 emails/day)
3. In Gmail, compose a new email
4. Paste Template A, B or C based on the `Language` column
5. Click the GMass button → Connect to your Google Sheet
6. Map columns: {{First Name}} → First Name, {{Company}} → Company
7. Set Send As: individual emails (not bulk), 10/hour max to avoid spam filters
8. Schedule: send Mon–Thu 9–11am (best open rates)

## Tips

- Personalise the first line with something specific to each company where possible
- BCC yourself on the first 5 to check formatting
- Follow up once after 7 days if no reply — one follow-up doubles response rate
- Track opens with GMass built-in tracking
