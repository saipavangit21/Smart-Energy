## 3. Posting batch — personal profile, professional tone

LinkedIn tone differs from the Facebook group posts: less "personal story," more direct value statement, can include a data point or question to drive comments. Post 2-3x/week, space at least 2 days apart. Always post from your **personal profile**; tag/mention the SmartPrice.be page so it can reshare.

### Post 1 — Founder story / why you built it (EN)
```
Two years ago I started tracking Belgian electricity prices out of curiosity. What I found surprised me: prices can swing from €0.04/kWh to €0.32/kWh — within the same day, on the same EPEX market everyone's dynamic contract is priced off.

Most people have no visibility into this. Most companies reimbursing employees for home EV charging don't either — they still use a fixed quarterly CREG rate that, by law (CIR 92), is supposed to reflect actual cost.

So I built SmartPrice.be: live EPEX prices for Belgium, a charge planner for EV drivers, and a free audit tool for fleet managers to see exactly what they're overpaying.

It's free. No subscription wall on the core data. smartprice.be

Curious — if you manage a fleet or just charge an EV at home, how do you currently decide when to charge?
```

### Post 2 — Fleet/HR audience, data-driven hook (EN)
```
Quick question for fleet managers and HR teams with EVs in the company car policy:

How is your home-charging reimbursement calculated?

If the answer is "a fixed rate from CREG," there's a gap worth knowing about: Belgian tax law (CIR 92) requires reimbursement to reflect the actual cost at the time of charging — not a quarterly average. A fixed rate doesn't satisfy that, and it leaves no audit trail for your social secretariat.

We built a free tool that calculates the exact gap: smartprice.be/fleet-audit
Takes under 2 minutes, no signup to see the result.

Happy to share what we're seeing across different fleet sizes if useful.
```

Belgium had electricity prices go negative for 24 hours this week — meaning the grid effectively paid consumers to use power.

We track this live at smartprice.be — there's a running counter of negative-price hours per week.

Anyone here actually timed an EV charge or appliance around a negative-price window?
